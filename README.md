# BoostBuddy

BoostBuddy is a premium client account management and social media campaign portal. The platform enables businesses to purchase secure virtual browser profiles ("Boxes") and automate targeted Facebook review campaigns with a credits wallet system and load-balanced employee assignment queues.

---

## Core Product Features

### 1. Virtual Browser Profiles ("Boxes")
Clients can subscribe to virtual browser profiles ("Boxes") for dedicated account operations. 
*   **Plain Configuration:** Virtual box assignments, login details, and 2FA secrets are configured by admins and made available directly in the client dashboard.
*   **Flex Subscriptions:** Clients can purchase boxes on dynamic packages, renewal terms, and upgrades with automated price proration.
*   **Security Controls:** Clients can submit requests to request credentials changes directly through the dashboard.

### 2. Multi-URL Reviews System
Allows clients to run reviews campaigns targeting multiple pages or posts in a single order.
*   **Single-Source Setup:** Write the review text or upload photo proof once, and apply it dynamically across multiple Facebook links (up to 10 URLs per order).
*   **Flexible Campaign Types:**
    *   **Standard Reviews:** High-quality text reviews written and posted by employees (costs 10 credits/unit).
    *   **Reactions & Comments:** Simple emoji reactions and comment text (costs 5 credits/unit).
    *   **Photo Reviews:** Advanced reviews combining text and photo uploads (costs 20 credits/unit).
*   **Gender Filter:** Target campaigns specifically to Male or Female profiles to match target audiences.

### 3. Concurrency Credit Wallet
A unified transactional credit wallet for purchasing and spending platform credits.
*   **Credit Packages:** Clients purchase credit packages (ranging from 10 to 250 credits) securely via card payments to top up their wallet balance.
*   **Auto Deduction:** Creating review campaigns automatically checks and deducts credits from the user's wallet.
*   **Transaction Ledgers:** A detailed transaction ledger shows audit logs of purchases, campaign spending, refunds, and adjustments.

### 4. Smart Load-Balancing Task Assignment
An automated task queue that distributes review orders fairly among available workers.
*   **Workload Optimization:** New campaigns check employee workloads and auto-route tasks to the active worker with the fewest in-progress assignments.
*   ** Telegram Dispatcher:** Instantly notifications employees via Telegram when new work is assigned.

### 5. Multi-Channel Notifications
Dispatches alerts and status updates using multiple channels.
*   **Telegram Bot Alerts:** Delivers real-time notifications for order acceptances, completion proof uploads, and account changes.
*   **In-App Alerts:** A clean notifications history interface across all dashboards.

### 6. Billing & Invoice History
*   **Italian SDI Compliance:** Supports individual or company billing profile setups, verifying tax IDs, VAT numbers, and Italian SDI codes.
*   **PDF Invoices:** Allows admins to upload custom invoice PDFs directly linked to orders, letting clients download them securely.

---

## User Portals & Dashboards

### 👤 Client Dashboard
A complete client portal to manage profiles and campaigns:
*   View credentials and 2FA codes for active browser boxes.
*   Order reviews, manage active campaigns, and track completion progress per URL.
*   Top up credits wallets and view transaction ledgers.
*   Configure billing address data and download PDF invoice histories.

### 💼 Employee Portal
A simple dashboard for remote workers to handle assigned tasks:
*   Toggle availability status to automatically receive new review tasks.
*   Claim available review tasks and view detailed instructions, review text, and photos.
*   Upload completion proofs (screenshots) to verify tasks.
*   Monitor stats and order completion history.

### 👑 Admin Control Panel
An administrative interface to oversee platform operations:
*   Deploy, manage, and assign virtual browser profiles (boxes).
*   Approve, modify, and monitor review campaigns and URL-level tasks.
*   Verify employee completion proofs and track workloads.
*   Manage clients and employees (activate, freeze, or deactivate accounts).
*   Upload PDF invoices and manage system settings.
